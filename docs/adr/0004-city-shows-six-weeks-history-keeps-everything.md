# The city is six weeks; the history is the whole record

Size follows about six weeks of training, and a quiet district or sub-district shrinks, because the city is the shape of recent training. Nothing leaves the map. A pool-only swimmer still has a lake. A district remains the door to the athlete's Garmin history: every session, and the statistics that sit with them, including sessions the skyline no longer reflects.

A tiny lake can still hold years of open-water swimming. Size is the recent picture. It is not a copy of the archive. Garmin holds that archive.
