# Bike splits; Run and Strength do not

Indoor cycling and road cycling are sub-districts of Bike, each with its own size, on the same terms as Pool and Open water. A winter on the trainer builds Indoor and leaves the roads as a remnant. Opening either one opens the Bike record filtered to it.

Road cycling and a generic cycling label go to Road. Indoor cycling and a virtual ride go to Indoor. Gravel, mountain, and cyclocross do not go to Road.

Run stays a single district. Road, trail, treadmill, track, and virtual runs enlarge it together. Strength stays a single district. Splitting those two would fill the city with places that do not change how an age-group season reads.
