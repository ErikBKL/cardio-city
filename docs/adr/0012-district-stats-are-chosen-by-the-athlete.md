# District stats are chosen by the athlete

The district record shows measures for that district's whole Garmin history, not for the six weeks that size the skyline. The first open computes them from Garmin and caches the counters behind them: session count, total time, a count per effort zone, and, for an optional measure, a sum and a count. Averages and zone shares are those counters divided. A later sync adds any mapped session whose Garmin id is not already in the tally, however old that session is. The id is all we keep to avoid counting it twice. A refresh throws the counters away and computes them from Garmin again. Later opens do not rebuild them from the full history on their own. A session's zone is the zone where Garmin recorded the most time. Sessions with no zone data are left out of that share. Each district has its own shown set. The athlete can hide any default and can show other measures Garmin already provides, such as average cadence and vertical oscillation. The first time a stat is shown, its counters are seeded from Garmin once, and later sessions add to them. Those choices do not change the size module.
