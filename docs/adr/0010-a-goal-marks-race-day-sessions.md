# A goal marks the sessions on its date

A goal names a race, a local date, and the districts it uses. A half ironman uses Swim, Bike, and Run. The date and the six-week city window both use the athlete's local calendar. Each goal has its own countdown on the city, and the athlete chooses which countdowns to show. That countdown is the reminder. V1 does not send push or email. Hiding a countdown hides that goal's reminder. The goal still marks race day. Sessions on that date in those districts become competitions without a separate tap. A strength session that day stays ordinary. The athlete can mark or unmark any session.

When Garmin sends the race as separate legs plus one multisport file, the legs are the competitions. The multisport file stays on the goal and does not add hits to the weekly count.
